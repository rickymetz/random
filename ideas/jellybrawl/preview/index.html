About

A living-room party game. The TV is the shared screen; everyone plays on their own phone, with a selfie (or a doodle) as the face of their jelly blob. Minigames are free-for-alls, 2v2 to 4v4 team games and lopsided 1-vs-the-rest showdowns, strung together as a playlist, a Mario Party-style board, or a WarioWare-style gauntlet.

  • 29 minigames: flappy free-for-alls, blob soccer, a sniper hunting look-alikes, a kaiju in a neon city, tug of war, a bomb to defuse with the manual split across phones, drawing and voting, tilt mazes…
  • Phones do more than buttons: sticks, trackpads, tilt, drawing, private maps and secret info.
  • Three ways to play: a playlist where last place picks the next game, three boards with forks, shops and duels, and a microgame gauntlet with bosses.
  • Nobody sits out: knocked-out players drop goo on the living and send emoji.
  • Grit: neon on black with a CRT filter (which you can turn down or off).
  • Accessibility settings: reduced motion, text size, colour-blind shapes, brightness, CRT level.

Download & run

No install for players: phones just open a web page. To host with real phones, run the relay on any computer on the same Wi-Fi (Node 18+, no dependencies):

git clone https://github.com/rickymetz/random
cd random/ideas/jellybrawl
node server.mjs

It prints a TV link and a phone link; the TV shows a QR code for the phones.
