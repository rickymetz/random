Everything below is somebody else's work. Here is whose.

The catalogue

Public Domain TorrentsSource

Every title, genre, format and star rating here was read from their site. They have been collecting and distributing public domain film since the early 2000s, which is the entire reason this page has anything to show.

The scrape was run once, gently — sequential requests spaced 1.5 seconds apart with an identifying User-Agent, and every response cached so refreshes cost them nothing. If you find something you like here, go and get it from them; the downloads, the plot summaries and the comments all live on their pages, not on this one.

Years and artwork

The source site publishes no release year and no poster art, so both were filled in from three databases that do.

TheTVDBArtwork & years

Metadata and artwork provided by TheTVDB, and the largest single contributor of posters here. TheTVDB is built by volunteers who add and correct entries by hand. If a film is missing or wrong, you can add the information yourself, or support the project by subscribing.

fanart.tvArtwork

Additional poster artwork provided by fanart.tv, a community that curates high-quality artwork for films and television.

Wikidata and Wikimedia CommonsYears & artwork

Release years came mostly from Wikidata, whose data is released under CC0. Where neither TheTVDB nor fanart.tv had a poster, the image is served from Wikimedia Commons. Commons files carry their own individual licences and author credits, which are shown on each file's page.

How titles are matched, and where it breaks

The source site gives a title and nothing else — no year, no IMDb id, no identifier of any kind. So years and posters are matched to the databases by title alone. Two rules keep that from going badly wrong:

  • A candidate must match the title exactly, once case, spacing and punctuation are normalised. Without this the databases' fuzzy search quietly returns some other film, which looks identical to a real hit.
  • Candidates dated after 1990 are rejected. This catalogue's newest genuine entries are cheap late-eighties pictures, so a newer match means the real film simply isn't in the database and we have landed on a modern namesake.

It still isn't perfect. A remake from the same era can satisfy both rules, so some years and posters on this site belong to the wrong film. Films where no confident match was found show no year and get a poster drawn from their title instead of a borrowed one — a gap is more honest than a confident mistake.

What this page deliberately does not do

  • No plot summaries. The source site's synopses aren't ours to republish.
  • No download links. Every film card links to its page on Public Domain Torrents; the files stay with them.
  • No screenshots taken from their server.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no cookies. The page is a single HTML file and a JSON index.

About the films

These titles are catalogued as public domain in the United States, generally because their copyright lapsed or was never renewed. Public domain status varies by country and is occasionally disputed for individual films — the listing here reflects the source site's judgement, not legal advice.

Corrections

Wrong poster, wrong year, missing film? The fix usually belongs upstream: film metadata at TheTVDB or Wikidata, artwork at fanart.tv, and the catalogue itself at Public Domain Torrents. Re-running the scraper picks up whatever you improve.
