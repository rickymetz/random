The catalogue

Public Domain TorrentsSource

Every title, genre, format and rating here came from their site. They have been collecting public domain film since the early 2000s, back when almost nobody was.

We read their catalogue once, slowly. One page at a time, a second and a half apart, with a User-Agent that says exactly who is asking. Every page cached, so we never have to ask twice.

The films live with them. So do the summaries and the comments. Find something here, then go and get it there.

Years and artwork

The catalogue does not record a year. Or a poster. So we asked three databases that do.

TheTVDBArtwork and years

Most of the artwork here is theirs. TheTVDB is built entry by entry, by volunteers, by hand.

Spot something wrong? Fix it at the source and it turns up here on the next pass. Or subscribe, and help keep the project running.

fanart.tvArtwork

More posters, from a community with strong opinions about what counts as good artwork.

Wikidata and Wikimedia CommonsYears and artwork

Nearly every year comes from Wikidata, released under CC0.

When neither of the others has a poster, Commons fills the gap. Every file there carries its own licence and its own credit, on its own page.

Ratings

The catalogue carries its own star ratings, but only for about half the films, and three quarters of those sit at four or five stars. Not much to sort by. So ratings come from IMDb where a film can be matched.

IMDbRatings

Information courtesy of IMDb. Used with permission. Ratings come from their public data export, matched by the IMDb ID that Wikidata holds for each film.

Where no rating exists, the catalogue's own star stands in. The two are never mixed: a rating out of ten and a five-star vote from one small site measure different things, so they are shown on their own scales and the ten-point ratings sort first.

Matching

A title. That is all the catalogue gives us. No year, no IMDb ID, nothing else to go on. So we match on the title alone, carefully.

  • Exact, or nothing. Case, spacing and punctuation are levelled first. Skip this and a fuzzy search hands back a different film entirely, looking every bit as convincing as the right one.
  • Nothing after 1990. The newest films here are cheap late-eighties pictures. A more recent match does not mean we found it. It means we found something else wearing the same name.

It is not foolproof. A remake from the same era clears both rules, so some years and posters here belong to the wrong film.

When we are not confident, we say nothing. No year. And a poster drawn from the title, rather than borrowed from a stranger.

What we don't do

  • No plot summaries. They are not ours to reprint.
  • No download links. Every film points home to Public Domain Torrents.
  • No screenshots lifted from their server.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no cookies. One page and one data file.

About the films

These are catalogued as public domain in the United States. Copyright expired, or was never renewed.

Status varies by country, and for a few films it is genuinely contested. What you see here reflects the source site's judgement. It is not legal advice.

Found a mistake?

Wrong poster. Wrong year. Missing film. Every one of them is fixable, and the fix belongs upstream.

  • Film data — TheTVDB or Wikidata
  • Ratings — IMDb
  • Artwork — fanart.tv
  • The catalogue — Public Domain Torrents
